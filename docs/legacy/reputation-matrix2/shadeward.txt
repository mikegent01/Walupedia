Current contents include (partial inventory, Wario refuses to fully catalog it): approximately 47,000 gold coins in mixed currencies, seventeen cursed monkey paws ("collectibles!"), five Bomb Gift Boxes (live and armed), a mirror that shows alternate timeline versions of whoever looks into it (stolen from Raventree Manor), multiple bags of "definitely not stolen" Koopa Troopa shells, what Wario claims is "the original recipe for Peach's cake" (a stained piece of parchment with flour handprints), several bottles of expensive vampire wine looted from dinner parties, a crowbar labeled "PROPERTY OF IRON LEGION - RETURN IF FOUND", emergency rations (mostly garlic), and an alarming number of magical items of uncertain provenance.

Additional contents discovered through various incidents: three fake mustaches (for disguises that never work), a collection of IOUs from people who definitely aren't going to pay him back, a wanted poster of himself that he keeps as a "trophy", four healing potions (expired, but Wario insists they're "probably still good"), a set of lockpicks, various grenades and explosives, a grappling hook, fifty feet of rope (stolen from a ship), a bottle of invisible ink, a jar of extremely pungent cheese (forgotten and now possibly sentient), and what appears to be a small fortune in stolen jewelry.

The bag also contains Wario's "emergency fund"—several thousand gold pieces separated into a special compartment that he only touches in absolute desperation (defined as "situations where I might actually die before I can spend the rest of my money"). This fund has been dipped into exactly three times in eighty-five years, which Wario considers a point of pride.

Wario treats the bag as his mobile vault, and losing it would be psychologically devastating to him—possibly more so than actual physical injury. He checks it obsessively, counts his gold daily (it takes about an hour), and sleeps with it tucked under his head (which is uncomfortable but necessary because he trusts literally no one, including his own employees).
