# Story plan — The Feywild House (after "The Feywild House and the Open Door")

GM planning sheet for the chatroom AI. **The GM decides what is true; the AI only
plays reactions, dialogue and texture.** Items marked *(proposed)* are
suggestions to confirm or replace.

Picks up from `the_feywild_house_and_the_open_threshold` (TC:1035-09-03/MAT):
Beta Luigi has just opened the door.

---

## 1. The truth (GM eyes only — the AI never states this early)

1. **The real Luigi is dying.** After the lava-moat tunnels he was taken to Dr.
   Toad's Star Hill Clinic. His burns were filed as "serious but treatable",
   but they have turned bad, and conventional treatment is failing.
2. **The rumour:** royal Boo ectoplasm can heal even the deepest wounds.
3. **E. Gadd acted on it.** He captured **Prince Boo**, the King's son, and
   sealed him inside a modified containment tank in his vacuum. He has been
   extracting the ectoplasm for his research. **One vial is already gone**,
   sent to "Dr. D." (Dr. Dario). That vial is what saves Luigi (see §8).
4. **King Boo is a father hunting a kidnapper.** He has been dragging people
   into paintings to interrogate them: "Who took my son?" Mario was one of
   them. The paintings were never about Mario.
5. **Why Mario got out:** the night the Prince was taken, the royal frame magic
   faltered and Mario's canvas cracked. He walked out of the painting and
   out of the mansion's inner wing. King Boo was too busy searching to notice.
6. **Why Egadd's vacuum keeps failing:** it is running at capacity with the
   Prince inside, and the Prince keeps fighting the seal.

Egadd is not a villain. He is a desperate old friend who did something
monstrous for a good reason. Players should be able to arrive at either
judgement.

---

## 2. Standing rules for the AI

- **Party:** Wario, Waluigi, Mr. T, Beta Luigi, Jory Dobbs. NPC guide: E. Gadd.
- **Marching order:** the party chooses it in the Foyer. The AI must restate it
  at every room entry and keep it fixed. Traps hit the **front**, ambushes hit
  the **rear**, and the split (§4) cuts the line in half.
- **Only the current room exists.** The AI is handed the current room's entry
  below plus these rules. It never names rooms the party hasn't reached, and
  never reveals §1.
- **NPCs come and go per room.** Each room lists who is present. Wandering
  ghosts can show up anywhere. Not every room has to use the ghost listed.
- **Keys:** the AI tracks a key ring. A locked door stays locked until the
  party holds the named key.
- **Clues:** the AI hints, describes and lets players draw conclusions. It
  never explains a clue for them.

## 4. Fixed beats (must happen, in this order)

1. **Foyer:** Egadd greets them at the door. The party picks a marching order.
2. **Area 2, Graveyard (Bogmire):** **Egadd's vacuum breaks.** He insists on
   fixing it alone. Whoever is last in the marching order glimpses a **prince's
   crown in his bag.** Egadd shuts the bag and changes the subject.
3. **Area 3, Twins' Room (the middle):** the twins' hide-and-seek **splits the
   party**: the front half of the line and the back half. Each group plays the
   next rooms on its own.
4. **During the split:** **Jory Dobbs is taken by Boos** and leaves the party.
   His painting later hangs in the Artist's Studio.
5. **Fortune-Teller (Area 3 return):** the groups reunite.
6. **Area 4, Breaker Room:** the blackout. Uncle Grimmly wakes.
7. **Area 5, at the Secret Altar gate:** **Mario is already out.** The reunion
   cutscene (§7) plays, then some banter.
8. **King Boo attacks.** This is the final battle.
9. **After the battle:** the paintings release and **Jory is freed.** The truth
   comes out (§8).

## 6. Prince Boo clue trail (replaces Mario's glove, shoe and star)

Each clue says one thing. None of them says "Egadd did it".

| # | Where | What they find | What it points to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Projection Room | A home movie: King Boo teaching a small crowned Boo to haunt | There is a prince |
| 2 | Twins' Room | The twins say the Prince always won hide-and-seek, "but now he's hiding in a box that hums" | He was taken in a machine |
| 3 | Observatory | A star chart marks the night "the little crown went dark" | The date it happened |
| 4 | Nana's Room | Nana gossips that royal ectoplasm "mends any wound, dearie" | The motive |
| 5 | Balcony (2F) | A torn Star Hill Clinic chart: *Luigi — deteriorating* | Who needs mending |
| 6 | Telephone Room | Every phone replays King Boo asking each ghost "Who took my son?" | The paintings are interrogations |
| 7 | Sealed Room | Egadd's sealed lab: extraction notes, a rack with one vial missing, a note addressed to "Dr. D." | Egadd, and the vial's destination |
| — | Egadd's bag | The crown (Beat 2) | Ties it all together |

The Fortune-Teller can read clues 1–3 and give a prophecy that only makes sense
afterwards (for example, "The one who saves is the one who stole").

## 8. Ending

- **King Boo is beaten.** The paintings release, and Jory is freed along with
  the other people King Boo had taken.
- **The reveal:** the Sealed Room clues plus the crown force the confrontation.
  Egadd admits it, and the Prince is in his vacuum tank.
- **The players choose:**
  - **Free the Prince.** King Boo's war ends and he owes the party. Egadd loses
    any further supply.
  - **Keep him.** More ectoplasm, but King Boo becomes an enemy for life.
- **Either way, the vial already sent to Dr. D. stands.** That ties into the Dr.
  Dario storyline: it is what heals Luigi. Pick one:
  - (a) Dario uses it in 1035 on the dying Luigi and keeps the rest, which he
    uses again at the 1040 Waiting Room surgery.
  - (b) Dario holds it unused until the 1040 Waiting Room surgery.
